title: BR1-0001 average speed
---
A cyclist travels 12 km at 15 km/h and then 18 km at 9 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole journey?

Answer: The first leg takes 12/15 = 0.8 hours. The second leg takes 18/9 = 2.0 hours. Total distance is 30 km and total time is 2.8 hours, so the average speed is 30/2.8 = 10.71 km/h.

title: BR1-0002 pipes and cisterns
---
A tank is filled by one pipe in 6 hours and emptied by another in 9 hours. If both are open, how long does the tank take to fill?

Answer: In one hour the filling pipe contributes 1/6 of the tank and the draining pipe removes 1/9, so the net rate is 1/18 of the tank per hour. The tank fills in 18 hours.

title: BR1-0003 base rates
---
A test is 99% accurate and the condition affects 1 in 10,000 people. A patient tests positive. What is the probability they have the condition?

Answer: Just under 1%. In a million people, 100 have the condition and 99 are correctly flagged, while about 9,999 of the remaining 999,900 are falsely flagged. So 99 of roughly 10,098 positives are real.
