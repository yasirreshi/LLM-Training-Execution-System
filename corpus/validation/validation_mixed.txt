title: Sediment transport
---
A river carries sediment in two ways that behave quite differently. Suspended load travels within the body of the flow, held up by turbulence, and responds almost immediately to a change in discharge. Bed load rolls and bounces along the bottom, moves only above a threshold shear stress, and lags the flow considerably.

The practical consequence is that a single measurement of sediment concentration taken mid-column during a flood tells you about the suspended fraction and almost nothing about the bed load, even though the bed load is what reshapes the channel. Reservoir siltation estimates built from suspended measurements alone are therefore systematically optimistic.

def moving_average(values, window):
    """Simple moving average, returning a list shorter than the input."""
    if window < 1:
        raise ValueError("window must be positive")
    if len(values) < window:
        return []
    total = sum(values[:window])
    out = [total / window]
    for index in range(window, len(values)):
        total += values[index] - values[index - window]
        out.append(total / window)
    return out
